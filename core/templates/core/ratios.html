Interpretation guide

  • Current ratio: current assets divided by current liabilities.
  • Quick ratio excludes inventory from current assets.
  • ROE and ROA depend on reliable profit and balance sheet classifications.
  • Debt/equity is meaningful only when debt and equity are classified consistently.

These are indicative calculations. Industry, accounting policy, averages and seasonality affect interpretation.
